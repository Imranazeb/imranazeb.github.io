## Analysis
Pasqal Holding SA is a French-born quantum computing company that operates within the Technology sector under the Computer Hardware industry. Unlike traditional chip makers, Pasqal focuses on neutral-atom quantum processors — a cutting-edge approach to quantum computing that uses arrays of individual atoms as qubits. The company has positioned itself as a serious player in the global race to achieve practical quantum advantage, partnering with governments, research institutions, and enterprises seeking next-generation computational power.

The quantum computing industry is experiencing a renaissance of investor interest, fueled by breakthroughs from companies like Google, IBM, and a growing cohort of specialized startups and public companies. Governments around the world, including those in the EU and U.S., are pouring billions into quantum research as part of national technology strategies. Pasqal, with its European roots and neutral-atom technology differentiation, sits in a uniquely competitive niche within this rapidly expanding landscape.
